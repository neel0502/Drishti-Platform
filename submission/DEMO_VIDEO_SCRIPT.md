# Three-Minute Demo Video Script

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

“Crime records contain valuable connections, but officers lose time moving between tables and manually correlating cases. Drishti turns those records into an operational intelligence workflow.”

## 0:20–0:50 — Command dashboard

Open the dashboard. Explain the statewide snapshot, trend, and districts requiring attention. Emphasize plain-language decisions rather than technical scores.

## 0:50–1:15 — Map and hotspot

Open the crime map, filter by crime category and time, and select an affected district. Explain how this moves from a statewide alert to a specific operational area.

## 1:15–1:50 — Investigate

Search for the highlighted case or offender. Open the profile and related cases. Point out the evidence used for each proposed connection.

## 1:50–2:20 — Network intelligence

Open the network view. Filter the graph and show how officers identify a repeat-offender group without manually joining records.

## 2:20–2:40 — Action

Return to the situation alert and describe the recommended review or patrol response. State clearly that an authorized officer makes the final decision.

## 2:40–3:00 — Scale and impact

“Drishti is built for deployment on Zoho Catalyst. The production path adds indexed storage, role-based access, audit logs, and monitored data pipelines. Our goal is measurable: reduce investigation time while improving the quality and explainability of operational intelligence.”
