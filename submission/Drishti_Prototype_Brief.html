RENEGADES · DRISHTI01
THE PROBLEM

Critical information exists.
Operational context is fragmented.

Investigators and supervisors must manually connect facts across FIRs, people, evidence and case-status records before deciding what needs attention.

01

Disconnected FIR context

Names, phones, vehicles and narratives can remain isolated across cases.

02

Evidence without a shared view

Media and document updates are difficult to trace back to the wider case picture.

03

Delayed command response

Leaders need clear district-ready signals, not another system to manually reconcile.

The result: slower review, less explainable prioritisation, and missed connections between related records.
