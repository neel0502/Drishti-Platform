Drishti demo runbook

Follow this exact order while screen-recording. Show only the pages that support one clear investigation story; do not attempt to explain every menu item.

Open live Data Store deployment ↗
Before recording: Use the Data Store deployment link above, set browser zoom to 90%, close unrelated tabs, and wait for the dashboard data to finish loading. Use the opening PPT for the first 25–30 seconds, then switch to the live app.

Title slide

Purpose: Establish the problem and show this is a Catalyst-built solution.
ShowSlide 1, then Slide 2 from the opening/design deck.
Say

Drishti is an explainable intelligence workspace that connects FIR records, evidence and case progress. The challenge is not lack of information—it is fragmented context.

FIR Search

Purpose: Show the central entry point for investigative intelligence.
Go to / actionFIR Search → choose the validated scenario Operation Night Watch, or use the query: Show repeat burglary accused in Bengaluru at night.
Say

An officer can search by FIR number, person, phone, vehicle, offence, location clue or complaint narrative. Drishti returns reviewable FIR leads and repeat profiles—not automatic conclusions.

Intelligence Profiles

Purpose: Prove that linked records are brought together in one view.
Go to / actionOpen Intelligence Profiles. Select the repeat-offender result produced by the search, or use the Repeat-offender check scenario.
Say

Instead of making the investigator hunt across records, Drishti gathers the linked FIR history, identifiers and district context into a single profile for validation.

Link Analysis or Incident Reconstruction

Purpose: Demonstrate explainability and evidence gaps.
Go to / actionOpen Link Analysis to show shared phone, vehicle, narrative or co-accused links. Then open Incident Reconstruction and run the validated reconstruction scenario.
Say

Every connection is presented as an investigative lead with a record-based reason. Reconstruction separates what is recorded from what is inferred and flags missing evidence for officer review.

If time is short, show only Incident Reconstruction. It is visually stronger for the video.

FIR Registration & Evidence Intake

Purpose: Show a practical workflow, not only analytics.
Go to / actionOpen FIR Registration & Evidence Intake. Briefly show the FIR form, then upload one prepared image, PDF or short CCTV sample.
Say

Drishti supports structured FIR intake and evidence metadata. The prototype records the file details and checksum so the evidence workflow remains traceable.

Do not spend time filling every form field on screen. Show the workflow and one successful upload.

Case Progress

Purpose: Show the command and supervisory value.
Go to / actionOpen Case Progress → click Analyse lifecycle.
Say

This view identifies lifecycle bottlenecks and cases that may need supervisory review. It prioritises process attention; it does not judge a person or determine enforcement action.

Patrol Plan

Purpose: End with a practical district-level action.
Go to / actionOpen Patrol Plan → select Bangalore Urban → keep Balanced plan → click Plan deployment.
Say

The plan uses only FIR coordinates inside the selected Bangalore Urban boundary. Out-of-boundary coordinates are excluded, preventing misleading patrol zones outside the district.

AI Evidence & Confidence

Purpose: Close with responsible AI and Catalyst proof.
Go to / actionOpen AI Evidence & Confidence. If available, briefly show the data-source status and model/guardrail content.
Say

The prototype is deployed on Zoho Catalyst. AppSail hosts the application service, and the connected relational FIR development subset is read from Catalyst Data Store through ZCQL. All AI outputs are decision support and require officer validation.

Closing line

Drishti turns disconnected FIR intelligence into an explainable, reviewable workspace for investigation and command—built on Zoho Catalyst.
