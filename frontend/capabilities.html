What is available in Drishti?

A clear guide to the investigation workspace, its decision-support tools, and the AI agents available to officers. Every output is a lead, draft, or review aid—not an automated police decision.

Human decision required. Drishti can find, organise, explain, and draft. It cannot arrest, contact a person, merge cases, approve an action, or treat a pattern as proof.

Core workspace capabilities

These are the things an officer can use without needing to understand the technical system underneath.

⌕

Search a FIR

Find a case using its crime number, person, phone, vehicle, or recorded facts. Open the result and continue from the case workspace.

↔

Verify linked cases

Compare candidate FIR links with visible signals such as common accused, phone, vehicle, offence pattern, location, and time.

◷

Reconstruct the timeline

View recorded events in order and see what is inferred, missing, or needs a timestamp check.

!

Find evidence gaps

Identify missing custody, CCTV, route, victim, forensic, arrest, or chargesheet information before a review.

◎

Review people and networks

Explore recorded person profiles, repeat-case context, and relationships without converting a connection into an allegation.

▤

Plan investigation work

Create editable task drafts, set ownership and due dates, and send a clearly scoped item for human review.

⌂

Run a shift briefing

See priority cases, overdue reviews, pending handoffs, and data-quality issues in one source-linked briefing.

⌁

Coordinate districts

Prepare a bounded cross-district verification memo. A designated supervisor still decides whether any coordination occurs.

✓

Prepare for review or court

Build a supervisor brief, inspect documentation readiness, and flag unresolved case-file gaps.

How an officer uses an agent

Open the caseSearch the FIR or start from the shift and review queues.
Choose one taskFor example: verify links, find evidence gaps, or prepare court readiness.
Read the supportCheck the cited records, evidence signals, missing links, and limitations.
Make the human decisionVerify, edit a task draft, request a review, or reject the lead. The officer stays accountable.
